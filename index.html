Your health has three sides

The Health Triangle is made of three sides that support each other. When one side gets stronger — or weaker — the others feel it too. There's no "perfect" triangle, and balance doesn't mean equal thirds. It means giving attention to the side that needs it right now.

Physical
Movement, food, sleep, safety, taking care of your body.
Mental & Emotional
Handling stress and feelings, self-talk, coping, asking for help.
Social
Communication, boundaries, friendships, life online, support.
How this works & your privacy. You'll answer an honest self-check — 36 quick questions, twelve for each side of your triangle, so you get an accurate picture. Build a SMART goal

Need an idea? Tap a starter (from your Health I lessons):

Name the action clearly. Not "be healthier" but "walk after dinner."

Be specific with a number and a frequency: how many times per day or per week? "Sometimes" and "more" can't be measured — "4 times a week" can.

Pick a start you're confident you can actually do this week.

Your real reason keeps you going when it's hard.

Be specific: name the day or time of day you'll do it, and the date you'll check your progress. "Right after dinner, starting Monday" beats "later."

From Lesson 1.4: naming the obstacle now makes it easier to handle later.

Each time you check in, jot how the goal is going. It's stamped with today's date and added to this goal's Progress log, so you can look back later and see how you improved. Stays on your laptop.

If you ever need to talk to someone

Everyone's mental and emotional health matters. If you're struggling, reaching out is a strength, not a weakness.

988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text, 24/7)
SafeUT — free app to chat with a licensed counselor (Utah)
National Alliance for Eating Disorders — 1-866-662-1235
And a trusted adult — a parent, teacher, counselor, or coach — is always a good first call.
